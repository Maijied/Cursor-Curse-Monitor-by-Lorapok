Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Summary

Cursor Curse Monitor reads your Cursor session only. We do not operate a cloud service that stores or aggregates usage data.

What data is accessed

  • Your Cursor auth token from local state.vscdb (on your computer only)
  • Usage summary and profile from api2.cursor.sh using that token
  • Your extension settings (poll interval, budget cap) stored in VS Code/Cursor settings

Who can see your usage

  • You only — the dashboard shows data for the Cursor account currently signed in on this machine.
  • Not your team admin via this app — this extension does not expose other members' usage.
  • Team limits — if your plan is team-based, you may see team limit context returned by Cursor's API for your session, not a full team usage breakdown.

What we do not do

  • No usage data sent to Lorapok Labs servers
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No sale of personal data

Model fallback

When enabled, the extension may write Composer 2.5 (Fast off) into your local Cursor model config when limits are exceeded. This happens locally in state.vscdb.
