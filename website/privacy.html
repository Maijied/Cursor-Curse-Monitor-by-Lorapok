Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Summary

Cursor Curse Monitor reads your Cursor session only. We do not operate a cloud service that stores or aggregates usage data from the extension.

What data is accessed

  • Your Cursor auth token from local state.vscdb (on your computer only)
  • Usage summary and profile from api2.cursor.sh using that token
  • Your extension settings (poll interval, budget cap) stored in VS Code/Cursor settings

Who can see your usage

  • You only — the dashboard shows data for the Cursor account currently signed in on this machine.
  • Not your team admin via this app — this extension does not expose other members' usage.
  • Team limits — if your plan is team-based, you may see team limit context returned by Cursor's API for your session, not a full team usage breakdown.

Marketing website

  • Email subscribe — if you opt in on the homepage, we store your email address to send release notes and important notices. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • Anonymous analytics — the marketing site counts page visits and marketplace link clicks (sessionStorage may prevent double-counting in one session). Counters may be stored in Firestore and/or synced into public visitor-stats.json. No advertising cookies or fingerprinting.
  • Optional extension heartbeat — off by default. If you enable cursorCurseMonitor.anonymousUsageStats, see Terms for the install-ID ping. With the setting off, the extension does not phone home.

What we do not do

  • No forced extension telemetry (heartbeat is opt-in)
  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party ad trackers in the extension

Model fallback

When enabled, the extension may write Composer 2.5 (Fast off) into your local Cursor model config when limits are exceeded. This happens locally in state.vscdb.
