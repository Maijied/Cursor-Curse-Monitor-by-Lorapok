Features

Live dashboard

Sidebar panel + status bar with auto-refresh from Cursor API.

Budget cap

Set a personal USD limit and track on-demand spend.

Limit alerts

Warning at 80% (configurable) before you hit the wall.

Free fallback

Auto-switch to Composer 2.5 (Fast off) at 100% usage.

Your data stays yours

  • Only your login — usage is read from your Cursor auth token on your machine.
  • No shared dashboard — teammates cannot see your usage through this extension.
  • Team context — if you are on a team plan, you see limits returned for your account session (same as Cursor shows you), not other members' usage.
  • No telemetry — no analytics, no third-party tracking, no cloud storage of usage data.
