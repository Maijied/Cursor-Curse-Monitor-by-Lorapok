## Problem

Mail and audit paths still hit KV on every send even after D1/R2 migration. When the daily KV put quota is exceeded (~1,000 writes/day on Workers Free), sends and related operations fail instead of degrading gracefully.

## Fix

Centralized fallback system:

- **`kv-put.js`** — `putKvStringSafe` / `putKvJsonSafe` catch quota errors; `markKvWriteQuotaHit()` pauses until UTC reset
- **`mail-storage.js`** — `shouldBlockKvWrites(env)` checks flags, in-memory pause, and stats `writesPausedUntil` **before** hot-path writes
- **Mailbox / audit / system-log / usage** — D1/R2 primary; skip KV when quota blocked; R2 outbox as last resort
- **Outbound mail** — never fails due to KV write failure

Config/cron paths still throw on quota so stats-refresh can record `writesPausedUntil`.
